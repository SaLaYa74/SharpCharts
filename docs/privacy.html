Privacy Policy

SharpCharts Poster · effective November 10, 2026

SharpCharts Poster is a private automation tool. It publishes posts to, and reads statistics and comments from, three Instagram professional accounts that we own and run: @paycheck_math, @sharp_charts and @liftoff_daily. It is not offered to other people or businesses, and it never connects to anyone else's Instagram account.

What the tool accesses

  • Our own accounts: account ID, username and follower count.
  • Our own posts: the posts we publish and their statistics (views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares, follows and average watch time).
  • Comments on our own posts: the comment text, read only to see whether it names a U.S. state, so we can reply with that state's rank from the chart in the post.

What we keep

  • Post statistics are saved in reports on our own computer to see which posts work best.
  • For each comment we reply to, we keep the comment's ID, the time of our reply and the state abbreviation(s) it named. This only exists so we never reply to the same comment twice.
  • We do not store comment text, usernames, profile pictures or any other information about the people who comment.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • We don't use the data for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we don't combine it with other sources.
  • We don't send direct messages, and we don't contact commenters except by replying publicly to their comment on our own post.

Deleting your data

If you commented on one of our posts and want any record of it removed, send a direct message on Instagram to @paycheck_math, @sharp_charts or @liftoff_daily saying "delete my data". We will remove the stored ID of your comment, and our reply if you ask, within 30 days and confirm by message. Deleting your own comment on Instagram also deletes its text everywhere; we never had a copy of it.

Security

The access token for the tool is stored encrypted (in GitHub's encrypted secrets and the macOS Keychain) and is never written into code or files.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: send a direct message on Instagram to any of the three accounts above.
